The Story

After 23 Years Building
Everyone Else's Vision

Shatica Miles spent 23 years in corporate America. She showed up. She delivered. She trained every boss she ever had — and was never given the position.

She was the person everything ran through. The one they called indispensable. The one they called family — right up until it was time to pay her what she was worth.

"You show up for everyone. You hold it together. You fix it. You deliver. And somehow you are still the one who is drained."

— Myss T

She walked away and rebuilt her life from scratch. Three brands. A garden. A channel. An app. All of it built on one truth: you cannot keep pouring from an empty cup.

This is not self help talk. This is the conversation that was missing.

What We're Up Against
